Questions

Which tape archive software is best for a small business?

It depends on how much data you keep, your LTO drives, your operating system and who runs it. Type your setup into the advisor: it weights price, ease of leaving later and the features you need, then ranks 14 tape archive products with the Tape Archive Software Ledger scores.

Why does the tape format matter so much?

An archive often outlives the software that wrote it. Products that write LTFS or another open format leave tapes that other tools can read; a proprietary format means you need that product, or its vendor's tools, to get the data back. The advisor weighs this whenever you keep data for many years.

Where do the scores come from, and is the advisor vendor neutral?

From the Tape Archive Software Ledger 2026, which scores tape archive products on ten capability areas, vendor credibility, buyer risk and lock-in from public records only. TapeBackup.org is not paid by any vendor on the list. The ranking is the weighted average of those scores, with weights set by fixed rules from your answers; no AI model writes the answer.
