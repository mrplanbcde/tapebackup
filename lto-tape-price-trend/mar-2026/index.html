LTO Tape Prices: March 2026

LTO-8 and LTO-9 were the price to capacity sweet spots. LTO-10 drives and media carried an early adopter premium, while LTO-5 and LTO-6 drives were mostly refurbished.

Archived snapshot

TapeBackup.org LTO price summary and drive price guide, first published 16 March 2026.

This is an archived price snapshot and is no longer updated. For current prices see the September 2026 LTO price tracker.

Notes on this snapshot

  • Dell spare part list prices were far above street; third party and refurbished drives sold for $1,800-$2,500.
