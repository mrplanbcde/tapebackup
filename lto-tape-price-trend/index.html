What changed

How LTO cartridge prices moved in 2026

The January and March 2026 editions were compiled from different sources and rounded, so small differences are noise. The clear moves: LTO-6 and LTO-7 media got more expensive as supply thinned, LTO-8 and LTO-9 rose a little, and LTO-10 30 TB held steady.

Market context

What is moving the LTO market

  • Tape4Backup shows a site-wide banner: 'From September 1st, 2026, all LTO Tape prices have increased.' Despite that, its LTO-9 singles ($93.50-$99.50) and LTO-10 30TB singles ($256-$286.75) sit in line with other sellers. Source
  • Trend vs early 2026: LTO-9 cartridges flat (Symply quoted about $94 in Feb 2026; now $92-$112). LTO-10 30TB roughly flat to slightly down (Symply ~$280 in Feb 2026; now $256-$300). LTO-10 40TB lower than expected launch price (Symply expected ~$570 or $14.25/TB; now $491-$555 or $12.28-$13.88/TB). Symply blamed the 40TB premium on launch pricing, tight Aramid base film supply and hyperscaler deals. Source
  • DatacenterDisk (published 2026-08-17, data updated 2026-09-17): LTO tape 'largely stable at $4-$6/TB' while HDD prices rose sharply in 2026; tape's $/TB edge over HDD widened from about 2x to 3.5x. The stated reason is an independent supply chain (Fujifilm and Sony in Japan) that avoids the NAND shortage and hyperscale HDD demand. Source
  • LTO Program (Sept 2026): 160.3 EB of LTO capacity shipped in 2025 (down 9% from the 176.5 EB record in 2024); Q1 2026 shipped capacity up 57% YoY, driven by LTO-9 adoption and the LTO-10 ramp; 2026 expected to set a new record. Source
  • The Register (2026-09-02): only Sony and Fujifilm make LTO cartridges. Analyst Tom Coughlin tied the 2025 dip to 'the evolving international trade environment' making buyers more cautious (tariff-related caution, but no price figure given). Source
  • Blocks & Files (2026-09-04): suggests the 2025 dip may reflect buyers waiting for LTO-10; projects 2026 could reach about 251.67 EB if Q1 pace holds. Source
  • Fujifilm LTO Ultrium 10 40TB cartridge (16978170) uses an aramid base film and began shipping January 2026; it works in existing LTO-10 drives. IBM 40TB cartridges (models 564/664) available 2026-03-06. Source
  • Half-height LTO-10 drives: Symply support article said HH drives expected Q2 2026 (FH first because of the new head guide assembly). No HH LTO-10 drive was listed at any seller checked on 2026-09-17. Source

Method

How we collect LTO prices

We load product pages at specialist tape resellers (Tape4Backup, LTO World, BackupWorks, TapeandMedia, MagStor, DatacenterDisk and StoragePartsDirect in this edition) and record the listed price, part number and stock note. We exclude listings the seller marks as inaccurate and prices that look like listing errors. Amazon, B&H, CDW, Connection and the HPE Store blocked automated checks or showed no price, so big-box and used-market prices are not included. When a new edition is published, the previous one moves to the price history.

What is the cheapest LTO tape per terabyte in 2026?

LTO-9. New 18 TB LTO-9 cartridges were listed at $92.45 to $111.55, or $5.14 to $6.20 per native TB, slightly below LTO-8 at $5.83 to $6.67.

How much does an LTO-10 tape cost?

30 TB LTO-10 cartridges were listed at $256.00 to $299.99 and the newer 40 TB cartridge at $491.00 to $555.00 on 17 September 2026.

How much does an LTO-9 tape drive cost?

New internal half-height SAS LTO-9 drives were listed at $7,699 to $13,500 and external desktop SAS drives at $8,999 to $13,385.

Are LTO tape prices going up in 2026?

Cartridge prices are flat to slightly higher than in early 2026, while listed drive prices are well above the estimates published earlier in the year. One seller announced a general LTO tape price increase from 1 September 2026, but its prices still match other sellers.
