Tape Backup 5-Year TCO: Systems Compared

The tape backup systems with the lowest 5-year total cost of ownership, compared at 100 TB, 500 TB and 1 PB against cloud archive storage.

Over five years, an LTO-8 or LTO-9 system holding two copies is the cheapest way to keep 500 TB or more in the US: about $19,200 for LTO-8 and $26,900 for LTO-9 at September 2026 prices, against $27,660 for five years of Glacier Deep Archive before any restore. Below about 100 TB, cloud archive storage usually costs less unless you restore often.

Our model counts what you buy once: cartridges for two copies and the drives (one below 500 TB, two from 500 TB). Prices are midpoints of new US seller listings from the price tracker. The cloud figure is AWS S3 Glacier Deep Archive list pricing for US East, from our tape vs cloud 5-year cost page.

Tape's cost is front-loaded and then nearly flat, so the longer you keep the data, the better tape looks. A cloud archive costs the same each month and charges again to read data back: one full restore of 500 TB from Glacier Deep Archive with bulk retrieval and internet egress costs about $43,074 on top of storage.

What a full TCO also includes

Our numbers leave out costs that depend on your setup, so add them for your own case: backup software licences, a library or autoloader, power, offsite storage for the second copy, staff time, and a migration to newer media when the drives near end of support.

Power is the smallest of these for tape: see our energy and operating cost comparison. Software is often the largest, so compare tape features in the software finder.

Frequently asked questions

Is tape cheaper than cloud over five years?

From roughly 500 TB, yes in our model: two LTO-8 copies cost about $19,200 and two LTO-9 copies about $26,900, against $27,660 for five years of Glacier Deep Archive at 500 TB, before any restore fees.

What is not included in these tape costs?

Software licences, a library or autoloader, power, offsite storage, staff time and the migration at the end of the drive's life.

How many tape drives do I need?

One drive is enough for a small archive. From about 500 TB we count two, so a backup and a restore can run at the same time and a drive failure does not stop you.

When is LTO-10 the cheapest option?

Not at current prices. LTO-10 costs more per terabyte in media and drives; it is chosen for density in large libraries.
