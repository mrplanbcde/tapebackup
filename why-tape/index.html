LTO tape offers the lowest total cost of ownership (TCO) for long-term data storage. While initial hardware investment is required, the ongoing costs are minimal.

Longevity and Stability

LTO tape cartridges are designed for 30+ year archival life when stored properly in controlled environments. This makes tape ideal for compliance and regulatory requirements.

Media Stability

  • • No mechanical wear when stored

  • • Stable magnetic properties

  • • Resistant to bit rot

  • • Self-contained and portable

Comparison

  • • Hard drives: 3-5 year lifespan

  • • SSDs: 5-10 year retention

  • • Tape: 30+ year archival life

  • • Cloud: Dependent on provider

Air Gap and Ransomware Protection

The ultimate defense against cyber threats

Tape provides a physical air gap - when ejected from the drive and stored offline, tape cartridges are completely isolated from networks and cannot be accessed, modified, or encrypted by malware.

The 3-2-1-1-0 Backup Rule

  • 3 copies of your data

  • 2 different media types

  • 1 copy offsite

  • 1 copy offline (air-gapped) ← This is where tape excels

  • 0 errors after verification

Protection Against

  • ✓ Ransomware encryption

  • ✓ Malware propagation

  • ✓ Insider threats

  • ✓ Network-based attacks

Additional Security

  • ✓ Hardware AES-256 encryption

  • ✓ WORM (Write-Once-Read-Many)

  • ✓ Physical custody control

  • ✓ No internet dependency

Hybrid Backup Strategy

Tape works best as part of a comprehensive backup architecture

Modern organizations use a tiered approach combining multiple storage types to optimize for performance, cost, and security.

1

Disk (Primary Backup)

Fast recovery for recent data, immediate restore capability, typically 30-90 days retention

2

Cloud (Offsite Replication)

Geographic redundancy, disaster recovery scenarios, automated replication

3

Tape (Long-term Archive + Air Gap)

Cost-effective retention, compliance archives, ransomware-proof offline copies

Best Practice:

Use disk for rapid restores, cloud for disaster recovery, and tape for long-term retention and ultimate protection. This hybrid approach delivers the best of all worlds.
