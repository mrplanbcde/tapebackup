News roundup

LTO Tape News, September 2026

LTO tape news for September 2026: shipped capacity rose 57% year on year in the first quarter after a 9% dip in 2025, 40 TB LTO-10 cartridges went on general sale for about $490 to $555, half-height LTO-10 drives have still not reached sellers, and LTO-9 media prices stayed flat while hard drive prices climbed.

Prices

LTO prices in September 2026

LTO-9 cartridges cost $92.45 to $111.55 ($5.14 to $6.20 per TB) and 30 TB LTO-10 cartridges $256.00 to $299.99, checked on 17 September 2026. Media prices have been broadly flat through 2026, while hard drive prices rose sharply. Every listing is in the LTO price tracker, and earlier months in the price history.

News

What happened in LTO tape in September 2026

LTO shipments up 57% in the first quarter of 2026

The LTO Program reported 160.3 EB of LTO capacity shipped in 2025, down 9% from the 176.5 EB record in 2024, and a 57% year-on-year rise in the first quarter of 2026, driven by LTO-9 adoption and the LTO-10 ramp. It expects 2026 to set a new record.

LTO Program, 1 September 2026. Source

Why 2025 dipped: tariffs and buyers waiting for LTO-10

The Register quoted analyst Tom Coughlin linking the 2025 dip to caution over the international trade environment. Blocks and Files suggested buyers were waiting for LTO-10 and estimated 2026 could reach about 252 EB if the first-quarter pace holds.

The Register and Blocks and Files, 2 and 4 September 2026. Source

40 TB LTO-10 cartridges on general sale

The 40 TB LTO-10 cartridge, which uses an aramid base film and works in existing LTO-10 drives, is now listed by US sellers at $491 to $555, below the roughly $570 Symply had expected at launch. Fujifilm shipped first in January 2026 and IBM's cartridges followed in March.

TapeBackup.org price check and Symply, 17 September 2026. Source

Still no half-height LTO-10 drive

Symply had said half-height LTO-10 drives were expected in the second quarter of 2026, because full-height drives came first with a new head guide assembly. None was listed at any seller we checked in September, so LTO-10 remains a full-height, higher-cost option.

Symply support and TapeBackup.org price check, 17 September 2026. Source

Tape stays flat while hard drive prices rise

DatacenterDisk reported LTO media largely stable at about $4 to $6 per TB while hard drive prices rose sharply in 2026, widening tape's cost advantage per terabyte from about 2 times to 3.5 times. Our own September check found LTO-9 cartridges flat against early 2026.

DatacenterDisk, 17 September 2026. Source

Roadmap: LTO-11 planned at 70 TB

The roadmap the LTO Program refreshed in November 2025 still stands: LTO-11 at 70 TB native and LTO-14 at 365 TB, lower targets than before in favour of reliability and cost per terabyte. No LTO-11 date has been announced.

LTO Program, November 2025. Source

Are LTO tape shipments growing?

Yes again in 2026. The LTO Program reported 160.3 EB shipped in 2025, down 9% from the 2024 record, and a 57% rise in the first quarter of 2026.

Is there a 40 TB LTO-10 tape?

Yes. 40 TB LTO-10 cartridges from Fujifilm and IBM began shipping in 2026 and work in existing LTO-10 drives.

Are half-height LTO-10 drives available?

Not yet at the sellers we checked in September 2026. LTO-10 drives so far ship as full-height units.
