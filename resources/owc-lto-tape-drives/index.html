Drive guide

OWC Mercury Pro and Archive Pro LTO Drives

OWC now sells its Thunderbolt LTO drive, formerly the Mercury Pro LTO, as the Archive Pro in LTO-7, LTO-8 and LTO-9 versions with an IBM drive inside and Hedge Canister included, from $5,999.99 for LTO-7 to $12,499.99 for LTO-9 on OWC's US store; Archive Pro Ethernet shares a drive over 10 GbE, and there is no OWC LTO-10 drive yet.

The range

OWC's LTO drives today

OWC (Other World Computing) sells LTO tape drives in desktop enclosures, aimed mainly at Mac users and video teams. There are two current product lines:

  • OWC Archive Pro: a desktop LTO-7, LTO-8 or LTO-9 drive that connects over Thunderbolt, with an optional built-in SSD of 4 or 8 TB for staging data.
  • OWC Archive Pro Ethernet: the same idea as a network appliance, sharing the drive over 10 gigabit Ethernet (iSCSI), launched in April 2025.

The Mercury Pro LTO is the earlier name of the Thunderbolt drive. OWC's current manual is titled "Archive Pro LTO | Mercury Pro LTO" and calls Mercury Pro owners legacy users, so the Mercury Pro LTO-9 and LTO-7 models people search for are, in practice, sold today as Archive Pro. We found no OWC LTO-10 product as of 17 October 2026.

Compatibility

Which tapes each model reads

Archive Pro LTO-9LTO-8, LTO-9LTO-8, LTO-9
Archive Pro LTO-8LTO-7, LTO-8LTO-7, LTO-8
Archive Pro LTO-7LTO-5 to LTO-7LTO-6, LTO-7

The LTO rules are the same whatever the enclosure: an LTO-9 drive reads and writes LTO-8 and LTO-9 cartridges and nothing older. If your archive is on LTO-6 or LTO-7, check which model can still read it before you choose. LTO-7 is now three generations old, so the LTO-7 kit mainly makes sense for reading or extending an existing LTO-7 archive.

Reviews

What reviewers said

  • TechRadar Pro reviewed the Mercury Pro LTO-9 in 2022: tape economics look good, but managing an archive spread over many 18 TB tapes is harder than it looks, and the $6,299 price then did not include the ArGest licence.
  • Larry Jordan, a video editing trainer, tested a Mercury Pro LTO-8 loaner unit in 2021 and recommended it for media archiving despite the upfront cost.

We found no independent review of the current Archive Pro or Archive Pro Ethernet as of 17 October 2026. We have not tested these drives ourselves.

Who it suits

When an OWC drive makes sense

Good fit: a Mac-based video or photo studio that wants one box to plug in over Thunderbolt, with archiving software included and nothing to install inside a computer. Archive Pro Ethernet suits a small team that wants several editors to share one drive.

Look elsewhere: if you already have a server with a SAS port, a bare external SAS drive from HPE, IBM or Quantum costs less; if you need LTO-10, OWC does not offer it yet; if you need a library for many cartridges, see LTO tape libraries. Other desktop Thunderbolt brands are covered in the LTO drive buying guide, and other archive software, including YoYotta and Archiware P5, in the tape archive advisor.

Is the OWC Mercury Pro LTO still sold?

Under a new name. OWC now sells the Thunderbolt LTO drive as the Archive Pro, and its manual covers both Archive Pro and Mercury Pro LTO. LTO-7, LTO-8 and LTO-9 versions are listed.

Which drive is inside the OWC Archive Pro?

An IBM LTO Ultrium drive; the LTO-9 model uses an IBM Ultrium 9 mechanism. The original 2020 Mercury Pro LTO used an IBM LTO-8 drive.

How much does the OWC Archive Pro LTO-9 cost?

$12,499.99 on OWC's US store on 17 October 2026 for the kit with Hedge Canister, or up to $14,899.99 with an 8 TB onboard SSD. The Archive Pro Ethernet LTO-9 is listed at $14,999.99.

Does the OWC Archive Pro work with Windows?

Yes. OWC lists Windows 10 and 11 and Windows Server 2022 as well as macOS and Linux. It connects over Thunderbolt, so the PC needs a Thunderbolt port.

Does OWC make an LTO-10 drive?

Not as of 17 October 2026. The Archive Pro and Archive Pro Ethernet are sold with LTO-7, LTO-8 and LTO-9 drives.

What is the difference between Archive Pro and Archive Pro Ethernet?

Archive Pro connects to one computer over Thunderbolt. Archive Pro Ethernet sits on the network and shares the drive with several workstations over 10 gigabit iSCSI.
