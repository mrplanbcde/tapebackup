Why LTO Tape

Three reasons tape still wins the archive.

For storage teams managing data that must survive for years, LTO Ultrium remains the quiet workhorse behind the world's most resilient archives.

Capacity that scales

LTO density roughly doubles every generation. Grow your archive by adding cartridges to a shelf — not arrays to a rack. LTO-9 holds 18 TB native; LTO-10 jumps to 30 TB.

  • Open, interchangeable media
  • Vendor-neutral standard
  • Petabyte-class library support
Learn more

Security by design

A removed cartridge cannot be reached over the network. Pair that physical air-gap with hardware AES-256 encryption and write-once WORM media for true data immutability.

  • Hardware AES-256 encryption
  • WORM tamper-proof media
  • True physical air-gap
Learn more

Cost & sustainability

Idle tape draws no power. With the lowest cost per terabyte in storage and near-zero energy at rest, LTO keeps both your budget and carbon footprint down.

  • Lowest TCO per terabyte
  • Near-zero idle energy draw
  • Decades without forced migration
Learn more
